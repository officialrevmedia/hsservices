Airline Cutlery Packing

Cutlery solutions built for aviation.

Precision-prepared. Hygiene-compliant. Flight-ready.

Airline catering leaves zero room for error. H&S Services provides end-to-end cutlery preparation, from sanitization and sorting to roll-wrapping and tray packing, ensuring every set meets your airline's exact specification and hygiene protocols.

Our team is trained to handle high-volume packing cycles with speed and accuracy. We manage stainless steel cutlery sets including forks, knives, spoons and specialty utensils, carefully sorted by class of service (Economy, Business and First Class) and packed to your galley loading requirements.

Building Maintenance and Janitorial

Facility maintenance you can count on.

Keeping high-traffic, high-visibility facilities spotless, every day and every event.

H&S Services deploys fully equipped, professionally trained janitorial crews who operate on scheduled rotations and responsive deployment. We handle everything from pre-event set-up cleaning to post-event deep restoration, ensuring your venue is always presentation-ready.

Our supervisors conduct routine floor inspections, and our teams are equipped with commercial-grade machinery and eco-compatible cleaning products that meet current health and safety codes.

Commercial Laundry

Industrial-grade laundering for demanding environments.

Food-safe. Hygiene-certified. Delivered on time.

In food service and aviation, linen quality is not cosmetic. It is a hygiene imperative. H&S Services operates a dedicated commercial laundry facility exclusively for food and hygiene sector clients, processing tablecloths, napkins, blankets, pillows and face towels to the highest cleanliness standards.

We use industrial washing systems capable of processing high volumes while maintaining the strict temperature controls required to eliminate food residue, bacteria and contaminants. Each laundry cycle follows a documented process from soiled collection through wash, dry, press, fold and sealed delivery, giving your team a fully traceable hygiene chain.

Turnaround schedules are designed around your operation, whether you need overnight returns for the next day's airline service or same-week delivery for restaurant linen refresh cycles.

Items we process

  • Table napkins and tablecloths
  • Blankets and pillows (airline and hospitality)
  • Rolling face towels
  • Service uniforms and aprons
  • Kitchen towels and cleaning cloths
  • Seat covers and headrest covers
